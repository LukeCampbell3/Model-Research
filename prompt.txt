PHASE 4 — PROFIT SCORE
======================

Create:

sports/shared/profit_optimization/profit_score.py

Compute conservative profit score.

For each candidate:

break_even = market_side_break_even

raw_ev = model_probability - break_even
stress_ev = stress_probability - break_even
lcb_ev = lcb_probability - break_even

Compute normalized components using stable clipping/scaling so NBA and MLB are comparable without allowing one sport to dominate due to scale artifacts.

Base score:

profit_score =
0.35 * normalized_lcb_ev

* 0.20 * normalized_stress_ev
* 0.15 * forecastability_score
* 0.10 * structural_mispricing_score
* 0.10 * similar_state_tightness_score

- 0.05 * chaos_score
- 0.05 * board_readiness_risk_score
- failure_mode_penalty
- evidence_quality_penalty
- sport_uncertainty_penalty
- data_quality_penalty

Also compute:

* expected_value_units
* conservative_ev_units
* confidence_adjusted_ev
* downside_risk_score
* profit_score
* profit_score_reason
* score_component_breakdown

Rules:

* lcb_ev must dominate raw_ev.
* raw edge alone cannot create a top-tier play.
* Missing price means no defendable profit score.
* Invalid odds means rejection.
* Diagnostic-only evidence cannot become top-tier.
* MLB evidence quality must be explicitly penalized if gold/live evidence is thin.
* SAFE_STATE_CORE should boost score.
* SAFE_STATE_NEAR_CORE should be eligible but lower confidence than core.
* TRUE_UNSTABLE_STATE should force reject in NBA.
* NEEDS_MORE_SAMPLE should remain watchlist only.
* Price-dependent plays cannot become core unless actual current price clears all required thresholds.
* Candidates with high chaos or high board readiness risk cannot enter free board.

Decision tiers:

* REJECT_LOW_EDGE
* REJECT_HIGH_CHAOS
* REJECT_BOARD_RISK

Acceptance:
